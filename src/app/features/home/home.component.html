Small projects, meaningful change

Every object that gets reused is one less thing in a landfill — and one less new thing that needs to be manufactured, packaged, and shipped.

These are the goals behind every suggestion — reuse more, waste less.

  • Less landfill waste

    Keep usable materials in circulation instead of sending them to the bin.

  • Lighter footprint

    Reusing what you own avoids the cost of producing and shipping something new.

  • Mindful habits

    Seeing potential in old objects changes how you look at everything else.
